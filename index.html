Thursdays at Rise Beyond Hate

A safe space
to be heard.

Every Thursday, young people ages 13–21 come together in a supportive community — guided by licensed counselors — to talk honestly about how violence has touched their lives. They leave with people in their corner, real skills, and a plan for what's next.

What's coming next

Thursdays are
just the beginning.

Next comes the full Youth Leadership & Public Safety Academy — a semester of hands-on training across five pillars. Everyone on the waitlist is first in line.

01

Leadership & Civic Engagement

Public speaking, goal setting, teamwork, and how local government actually works — including mock public meetings.

02

Violence Prevention & Conflict Resolution

De-escalation, peer mediation, and bullying and hate prevention — and knowing when to bring in a trusted adult.

03

Public Safety & Emergency Preparedness

Emergency planning, fire safety, and CPR / First Aid certification — plus a look at careers in fire, EMS, and healthcare.

04

Career & Money Lab

Résumés, interviews, banking, budgeting, credit, and driver's-permit prep for real independence.

05

Community Impact Project

Tackle a real issue in the neighborhood, then present the solution to city leaders at the Youth Leadership Summit.

Graduates walk away with

  • CPR / First Aid certification
  • Documented service hours
  • A professional résumé
  • Financial-literacy skills
  • A mentor in their corner
  • Certificate of Completion

Meet the founder

Nehemiah Garley

Founder & Executive Director

As a foster parent, Nehemiah saw firsthand what stable relationships, mentorship, and positive adult guidance mean in a young person's life. He brings that to Rise Beyond Hate — along with four years on the Brooklyn Park Human Rights Commission, board service with CHOPS, Inc., and training in emergency management and First Aid / CPR.
